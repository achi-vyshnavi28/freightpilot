# Deccan Pharma transport agreement

Agreement DPL-TA-07 for temperature-controlled and general cargo. Rates are per trip in INR, exclusive of GST.

## Lane rates
| Origin | Destination | Vehicle capacity | Rate per trip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hyderabad | Bangalore | 7 T | 36,000 |
| Hyderabad | Bangalore | 15 T | 52,000 |
| Hyderabad | Chennai | 7 T | 37,500 |
| Hyderabad | Delhi | 15 T | 1,45,000 |
| Hyderabad | Visakhapatnam | 7 T | 33,000 |

## Temperature-controlled surcharge
Reefer vehicles carry a 22 percent surcharge on the lane rate. The surcharge applies only when the lorry receipt states the cargo as 2 to 8 degree cold chain.

## Detention charges
Detention beyond 12 hours is charged at Rs 3,000 per vehicle per day because of cold-chain compliance requirements.

## Billing disputes
Disputes must be raised within 10 days of the invoice date and copied to accounts payable.
