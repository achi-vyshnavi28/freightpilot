# Acme Steel freight rate contract 2026-27

Contract ACME-FRC-2026 between Acme Steel Pvt Ltd and the transporter, valid 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. Rates are per trip in INR, exclusive of GST, for full truck load.

## Detention charges
Detention beyond 24 hours at the loading or unloading point is charged at Rs 2,500 per vehicle per day. The first 24 hours are free. Detention must be supported by gate in and gate out times.

## Loading and unloading
Loading and unloading charges are payable only when the consignor requests labour from the transporter, at Rs 1,500 per trip.

## Billing disputes
Billing disputes must be raised in writing within 15 days of the invoice date. Undisputed amounts are payable within 30 days. Freight billed above the contract rate without written approval will not be paid.

## Fuel escalation
Lane rates are revised when the diesel price in Hyderabad moves by more than 5 percent from the contract base price of Rs 96 per litre.
